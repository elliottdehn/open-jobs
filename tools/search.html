Sort by my taste

1Where2What3Compare

In about three minutes the page learns what you actually want and re-orders every job by it. No account, no upload; it runs in this tab.

  1. Where — tick the arrangements and places that apply. Everything else is filtered out of the ranking.
  2. What — you'll see ~24 deliberately different jobs from that scope. Mark a few More like this / Less; skip the rest. This seeds the model.
  3. Compare — answer “which would you rather have?” about a dozen times. Each pair is chosen where the model is unsure, so every answer counts. It stops by itself once your ranking has settled.
Why pairs? People (and models) are bad at scoring jobs 1–10 but good at picking between two. Your answers become a taste vector that sorts the list; it's saved in this browser and exported with your labels.

How this page works

Jobs start ordered by how close they read to your ideal job description. Every label you give and every comparison you answer re-orders the unlabelled jobs, and the status bar tells you when that happens. Jobs you say Yes to move to a section at the top and stay in the order you picked them; No goes to a collapsed section at the bottom. Nothing leaves this browser except the enrichment calls you trigger.

Badges

Solid badges are stated in the posting or extracted from it mechanically. dashed badges are estimates from small models trained on the corpus, shown only where the posting doesn't say; they're separate filter values so they never masquerade as facts.
